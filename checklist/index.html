Requirements checklist

Aerodrome's requirements, and how Harvest meets them.

From the Autopilot ALM partner brief and Q&A, with Harvest's answer to each.

Hard requirementsSection 2

Aerodrome requiresHarvest
2.1Onchain-first vault state

Registry, pool, positions and ranges, deposits, share supply, fees, xAERO/AERO rewards, caps, strategy version and performance accumulators readable via view calls or events. The UI renders only from chain.
Yes

Every one of these is exposed through view functions and events. Aerodrome's UI needs no Harvest API or indexer.

2.2No emissions compounding

Never sell, swap or convert xAERO/AERO rewards into LP assets. Rewards flow to share holders per share, computable from chain.
Yes

The Autopilot vault passes xAERO through per share and never sells or compounds it. This is a dedicated build, separate from Harvest's existing auto-compounding vaults.

2.3Flexible fee routing

A fee on emissions and/or swap fees, configurable onchain, adjustable over time and per vault.
Yes

Fee share on emissions and swap fees is set per vault onchain and adjustable behind the parameter timelock.

2.4Vault caps

Per-vault total and per-user caps enforced at deposit. Raises need both Aerodrome and provider multisigs; lowers can come from the provider alone.
Yes

Caps in pool liquidity units, enforced in deposit. Raises require both multisigs; Harvest can lower alone. Open to a monthly review plus an 80% utilization trigger.

2.6ERC-4626 vaults

Transferable, composable shares; a share price resistant to one-block manipulation. Deviations argued and documented (e.g. ERC-7540).
Yes

ERC-4626 shares, transferable and composable with Aerodrome's gauges. Share price comes from vault accounting, not an external oracle, so it can't be moved within one block. Any extension will be documented.

2.8Single-chain vaults, full footprint

One vault per pool per chain, no cross-chain logic. Support every Aero chain now and new ones within about 30 days.
Yes

One vault per pool per chain. Harvest commits to every chain Aero runs on, and to new Aero chains within 30 days of launch.

2.9No mixing or routing to competing AMMs

No mixed share tokens, no routing Aerodrome LP capital to other venues. Disclose existing competing-DEX commitments.
Yes

Autopilot vaults hold Aerodrome positions only and route nowhere else. Existing commitments on other DEXes will be disclosed in full.

V3 architectural alignmentSection 2.7

Aerodrome requiresHarvest
2.7Continuous allocation

No epochs; per-second drip; gauge rates can change at any time.
Yes

Accounting accrues per second with no epoch assumptions anywhere in the strategy.

2.7xAERO redemption

Pass xAERO through to LPs or redeem on their behalf, with gas borne transparently by the LP.
Yes

xAERO is passed through to LPs in kind and claimable any time. LPs redeem to AERO themselves, so no hidden gas is charged.

2.7Lazy fee and emission settlement

Vault accounting reconciles against the gauge's lazily settled state.
Yes

The vault settles against the gauge on every state change and distinguishes fresh from lazily settled values in its views.

2.7Per-gauge emission caps

Strategy must not break when a gauge's rate drops mid-period.
Yes

Rate drops are a normal input to the staked/unstaked decision, never an error path.

2.7MEV-aware rebalancing

Rebalances must not leak value through vanilla AMM swaps; handle priority-fee and dynamic-fee conditions.
Yes

Rebalances are sized and timed around priority fees and dynamic pool fees, and abort on failed safety checks (slippage, value-loss bounds) instead of executing unsafely.

2.7Staked / unstaked liquidity

Document how shares coexist with the staked position NFT; support both modes with a sensible policy.
Yes

The vault holds the position NFT and shares represent a claim on it. Blue-chip pools stay staked; long-tail pools switch between fees and emissions within the policy we codify with you.

2.7TokenRegistry

Specify behavior for tokens not yet listed.
Yes

For unlisted tokens the vault runs unstaked in fee mode and stakes once the token is listed and the gauge is active.

2.7pauseChain / suspendChain

Specify behavior when governance pauses or suspends the leaf chain.
Yes

Rebalancing halts safely; deposits pause; withdrawals and emergency exits stay open.
